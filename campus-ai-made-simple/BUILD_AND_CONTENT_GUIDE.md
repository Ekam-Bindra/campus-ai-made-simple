## LinkedIn post: copy-ready current-status draft

What changes when you ask an AI assistant to show where its answer came from?

I’ve been exploring that through Campus AI Made Simple: a seven-lesson Gemini notebook built around a fictional campus assistant.

As an MIS student at UGA, I’ve learned a lot from workshops in Terry clubs. I wanted to turn some of that learning into something other students could try themselves.

The project moves from prompting and saved preferences to embeddings, retrieval, function calling, MCP, and a bounded agent workflow.

One result stood out: the assistant answered a biology exam question with the correct date, time, grade weight, and source citation. When I asked for a room number that wasn’t in the records, it said, “Not available in these records.”

Changing saved preferences from mornings to evenings also changed the suggested study sessions. It was a useful reminder that personalization depends on the context an application actually supplies.

I’ve verified the first four lessons live. The remaining three have local checks and are awaiting live verification. I’m documenting both the results and the limits as I go.

My goal is to make AI easier to understand, question, and use thoughtfully—and build toward helping other students learn through hands-on workshops.
